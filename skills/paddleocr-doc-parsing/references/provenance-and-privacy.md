The examples can call a hosted PaddleOCR API and may transmit document contents outside the local machine. Do not upload unpublished manuscripts, identifiable participant data, confidential peer-review material, restricted course records, or other sensitive documents unless the user has explicitly authorized the destination and the applicable privacy, ethics, and institutional requirements have been checked. Prefer an approved local workflow when those conditions are not satisfied.

Before updating, compare the upstream instructions and endpoint behavior, then preserve these privacy boundaries.
