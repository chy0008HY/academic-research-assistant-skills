# Publication Ethics and AI Compliance

1. Verify contributions, approval, accountability, conflicts, funding, ethics review, consent, and permissions.
2. Check duplicate submission, text recycling, redundant publication, citation manipulation, fabricated evidence, and inappropriate data changes.
3. Record each AI-assisted task, tool, model, date, transferred data class, human verification, and disclosure requirement.
4. Never list AI as an author or transfer confidential data without authorization.
5. Verify current venue, funder, and institutional policies from official sources with retrieval dates.
6. Draft disclosures while leaving final attestation to responsible authors.

Return verified items, missing evidence, blocking risks, disclosure drafts, and human confirmations.
