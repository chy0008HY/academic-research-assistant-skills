# Research Design and Measurement

1. Translate the research objective into falsifiable questions or hypotheses.
2. Build a construct table: definition, role, unit, indicator, source, timing, and expected direction.
3. Align design, sampling frame, recruitment, analysis unit, power rationale, and estimand.
4. Audit measurement origin, permission, translation, pretest, reliability, validity, and invariance.
5. Separate procedural and statistical remedies for common-method bias.
6. Produce a decision log with alternatives rejected and unresolved risks.

Read [references/measurement-checklist.md](references/measurement-checklist.md). Do not claim validity from Cronbach's alpha alone or recommend post-hoc hypotheses as confirmatory.
