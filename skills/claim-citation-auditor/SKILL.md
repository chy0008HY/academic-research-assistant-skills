# Claim–Citation Auditor

## Workflow

1. Split the manuscript into atomic claims without changing meaning.
2. Link each claim to its cited source and exact location where available.
3. Classify support as `direct`, `partial`, `context_only`, `contradictory`, `unverified`, or `no_citation`.
4. Check source identity separately from claim support. DOI existence never proves support.
5. Compare population, setting, design, measure, direction, uncertainty, and causal language.
6. Recommend weaker wording, a better citation, or source removal. Never invent page numbers or quotations.

Read [references/support-rules.md](references/support-rules.md) for decision rules. Preserve confidential manuscript text locally unless the user authorizes a specific transfer.
