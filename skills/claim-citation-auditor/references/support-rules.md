# Support rules

- Direct: the source tests or documents the same substantive relationship for a sufficiently similar construct and context.
- Partial: the source supports only part of a compound claim or a narrower population, direction, or mechanism.
- Context only: the source supplies background but does not test the claim.
- Contradictory: the source reports materially different or null evidence.
- Unverified: full relevant text or exact location is unavailable.
- No citation: a checkable empirical or historical assertion lacks a source.

Flag causal verbs when the cited design is cross-sectional or observational. Flag review articles used as substitutes for accessible primary evidence, while recognizing legitimate synthesis claims.
