# Framework routing

- Systematic review or meta-analysis: PRISMA 2020; protocol: PRISMA-P.
- Randomized trial: CONSORT and applicable extensions.
- Observational cohort, case-control, or cross-sectional study: STROBE.
- Qualitative interviews or focus groups: COREQ; broader qualitative reports: SRQR or APA JARS–Qual.
- Quantitative or mixed methods in behavioral and social science: APA JARS–Quant or JARS–Mixed.
- Prediction model: TRIPOD; AI-specific extension only when applicable.
- Web survey: CHERRIES as a supplement, not a substitute for design-specific guidance.

Always verify the current version and target journal policy from official sources. A checklist improves reporting transparency; it does not certify validity.
