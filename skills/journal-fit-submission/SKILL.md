# Journal Fit and Submission Strategy

1. Use the official website for current scope and rules; record URL and retrieval date.
2. Verify venue identity, article type, language, word limit, anonymization, fees, and data or AI policy.
3. Compare manuscript and recent venue content across topic, theory, method, contribution, audience, and geography.
4. Score fit as strong, plausible, or weak with evidence. Never guarantee acceptance.
5. Distinguish verified facts, dated facts needing recheck, author preferences, and inference.
6. Produce a ranked shortlist, adaptation plan, risks, and official links.

Third-party rankings and indexes do not replace official policy verification.
